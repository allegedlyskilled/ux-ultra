Welcome to Quayside Makerspace

We are a community workshop in the old net loft on Harbour Row. Members share woodworking, metalwork, textile and electronics tools, and anyone can join a class.

Membership

Monthly membership is £38 and gives 24-hour access after an induction. Concession membership is £22 for students and anyone receiving benefits. Day passes are £12.

Visit us

The Net Loft, 4 Harbour Row. Staffed open hours are Tuesday to Thursday 18:00-21:30 and Saturday to Sunday 10:00-17:00. Step-free access is through the side door on Pilot Lane.
